What’s on the Map

These are often called Land Registry compliant maps. Tailte Éireann (formerly the Property Registration Authority and Ordnance Survey Ireland) sets out how maps lodged with Land Registry applications should be prepared. Each map is drawn to those published requirements:

  • Ordnance Survey base mapping, supplied digitally by Tailte Éireann and left unaltered, with the boundary drawn on top so it’s clearly distinguishable from the mapping.
  • A red line around the property, plus any other colours your solicitor needs, such as rights of way or the part being transferred.
  • The right scale, usually 1:1000 for urban property or 1:2500 for rural land, as Tailte Éireann recommends.
  • ITM grid coordinates at the corners, with a north point, the scale and the county name.
  • A PDF sized for A3 or A4, ready for your solicitor to print and attach to the deed or application.

What to Send

The more you can send at the start, the quicker the map can be quoted and prepared:

  • The Eircode or address of the property
  • The folio number, if the property is already registered
  • Any existing folio map, deed map or title documents
  • A sketch or marked-up photo of the boundary or the part being sold
  • Any instructions or queries from your solicitor

Common Questions

How much does a Land Registry map cost?

A map for a house or site with one boundary starts from €195, a map for splitting a property from €245, and farmland or several parcels from €295. The Ordnance Survey mapping fee is included, and you’ll get a fixed quote before any work starts.

How quickly can I get my map?

It depends on the property and the map you need. Your quote will include a delivery date.

Will Tailte Éireann accept the map?

Maps are prepared to its published mapping requirements, but Tailte Éireann decides on each application. Your solicitor can confirm the map suits your transaction.

What’s the difference between a folio map and a deed map?

A folio map is Tailte Éireann’s own record map for a registered property, and you can get a copy from Tailte Éireann. A deed map is the map that goes with a transfer or application, showing the property or part of it being dealt with. That’s the kind of map I prepare.

Do you provide Declarations of Identity?

No. Your solicitor can advise on who should provide one if you need it.

Can you confirm where my legal boundary is?

No. The map shows the boundary as instructed by you or your solicitor. It doesn’t settle where a legal boundary lies, so please speak to your solicitor about any uncertainty or dispute.

Is the Ordnance Survey mapping included in the price?

Yes. The Ordnance Survey mapping fee is included in every price, so there’s nothing extra to pay Tailte Éireann for the base map.
